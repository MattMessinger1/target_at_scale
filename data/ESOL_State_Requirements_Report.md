# ESOL Certification & Training Hour Requirements by State

## Executive Summary

This report identifies states with mandated ESOL/EL certification or professional development hour requirements that represent high-value targeting opportunities for CARE Coaching. Based on the knowledge base research, **three states** have documented requirements:

| State | Key Requirement | Target Audience |
|-------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Colorado** | 45 clock hours CLDE | Content teachers with specific endorsements |
| **Florida** | 60-300 hours ESOL | Teachers, administrators, and staff |
| **Washington** | 100 clock hours | All educators for recertification |

These states have explicit, measurable PD hour requirements tied to licensure or compliance, making them ideal for systematic CARE Coaching outreach.

## Colorado

### Requirement Overview

The Colorado State Board of Education requires educators with specific endorsements to complete **45 clock hours** of Culturally and Linguistically Diverse Education (CLDE) training or professional development.

### Who Must Complete This Training

- Educators with **Elementary** endorsements
- Educators with **Math** endorsements
- Educators with **Science** endorsements
- Educators with **Social Studies** endorsements
- Educators with **English Language Arts** endorsements
- Educators with **middle-level** endorsements (e.g., middle school math grades 6-8, MIDL)

### Key Statutes & Regulations

| Reference | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| CO Statute 22-24-101 through 22-24-108 | English Language Proficiency Act (ELPA) |
| CO Statute 22-24-108(1)(b) | Requires "effective professional development activities related to teaching English language learners for all educators who may work with English language learners in the classroom" |
| State Board of Education Rules, Section 7 | Educator Licensing Rules for EL PD |

### CARE Coaching Opportunity

- **WCEPS is already an approved PD provider** in Colorado
- CARE Coaching qualifies as "Workshop" documentation for license renewal
- Districts/BOCES can contract for CARE Coaching and issue clock hour certificates
- Documentation should include: participant's name, workshop name, agency name, location, dates, contact hours, and provider signature

### Funding Sources

- **English Language Proficiency Act (ELPA)** funds - specifically for EL PD
- **At-Risk Funding** - weighted formula (12-30% above base per pupil)
- **Title II and Title III** federal funds

## Florida

### Requirement Overview

Florida has the most comprehensive ESOL training requirements, driven by the **1990 Consent Decree** and subsequent modifications. All instructional personnel working with ELLs must complete ESOL training, with hours varying by role.

### Training Hour Requirements by Role

| Role | Required Hours | Notes |
|------|---------------|-------|
| **ESOL Endorsement** | 300 hours | 5 strands × 60 hours each |
| **Content-area Teachers** | 60 hours | Must cover all 5 ESOL strands |
| **School-based Administrators** | 60 hours | Per 2003 Consent Decree Modification |
| **Guidance Counselors** | 60 hours | Per 2003 Consent Decree Modification |
| **Other Staff** (P.E., Music, Media Specialists, SLPs, Psychologists, Social Workers, World Languages, ROTC, OT/PT) | 18 hours | Must cover all 5 ESOL strands |

### The 5 ESOL Endorsement Strands

Training for all categories must address the **Florida Teacher Standards for ESOL Endorsement**, which contains five strands comprised of Standards and Performance Indicators. All instructional personnel who work with ELLs must integrate all strands, standards, and performance indicators into their daily curriculum.

### Key Compliance Deadline

**June 1, 2026**: School districts must submit their ESOL endorsement matrix to FL DOE for review and approval (new standards approved August 20, 2025).

### Key Statutes & Regulations

| Reference | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Rule 6A-6.0904(2)(g) | Requires "Basic ESOL instruction shall be provided by appropriately qualified personnel" |
| Rule 6A-6.0907 | Outlines 60/18 hour training requirements |
| FL Consent Decree (1990) | Original requirements for ESOL endorsement (300 hours) and in-service training |
| FL Consent Decree Modification (2003) | Added requirements for administrators and guidance counselors |

### Reference Links

- FL Rules and Legislation ELL webpage
- FL DOE English Language Learners/Professional Learning page
- FL DOE Student Achievement through Language Acquisition Bureau (SALA) - offers webinar series

### CARE Coaching Opportunity

1. **Comprehensive Support Grant (CSG) Schools**
   - Funding: ~$79,000 per eligible school
   - Schools identified for CSI, TSI, or ATSI
   - Many CSG applications have insufficient EL PD components
   - Schools can amend applications to include CARE Coaching

2. **60-Hour Course Development**
   - Opportunity to create a 60-hour CARE Coaching course covering all 5 ESOL strands
   - Would meet requirements for content teachers, administrators, and counselors

3. **Educator Certification Renewal**
   - CARE Coaching can provide in-service points for certification renewal
   - 1 semester hour = 20 in-service points

### Funding Sources

- **Comprehensive Support Grants (CSG)** - ~$39.3M statewide, $14M for underperforming subgroups
- **Title II and Title III** federal funds

## Washington

### Requirement Overview

Washington State has multiple overlapping requirements for educator PD related to multilingual learners, driven by state law and the **Transitional Bilingual Instruction Program (TBIP)**.

### Training Requirements

| Requirement | Hours/Status | Who It Applies To |
|-------------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Teacher Certification Renewal** | 100 clock hours | All educators |
| **TBIP Professional Development** | Required (mandatory) | Teachers, counselors, and staff in TBIP programs |
| **ELL/Bilingual Endorsement** | Required for assignment | Classroom teachers using TBIP funds |

### Key Statutes & Regulations

| Reference | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| RCW 28A.180.040(1)(f) | Requires districts to "Provide in-service training for teachers, counselors, and other staff, who are involved in the district's transitional bilingual program" |
| RCW 28A.180.040(2) | Requires ELL or bilingual endorsement for TBIP-funded teachers |
| WAC 392-160-010(3) | Requires "effective professional development training of sufficient duration and depth for administrators, teachers, counselors, and other staff" |
| WAC Chapter 181-85 | Clock hour rules for certification |
| WAC 181-85-107 | Documentation requirements for clock hours |

### Professional Learning Activities (per TBIP Guide Chapter 4)

Professional learning must include topics such as:
- Effective services for multilingual learners and AI/AN students
- WIDA English Language Development standards and assessments
- Second language acquisition
- Instructional strategies for English language development and accessible content
- Dual language research, program development, and instruction
- Culturally and linguistically responsive instruction for multilingual learners
- Core content/skills for ELL/Bilingual endorsed teachers

### CARE Coaching Opportunity

1. **Become an Approved Provider**
   - WCEPS should apply to become an approved clock hour provider through PESB
   - All Washington public school districts and ESDs are automatically approved providers

2. **TBIP Funding**
   - Can be used for ESL programs where bilingual instruction isn't practicable
   - Covers all EL/ML programs, not just bilingual programs

3. **Learning Assistance Program (LAP)**
   - LAP funds can be used for professional learning
   - Targets MLs not meeting academic standards

4. **Beginning Educator Support Team (BEST) Grants**
   - Annual application cycle opens Spring 2026
   - LEAs/ESDs must include CARE Coaching in grant applications

### Funding Sources

- **TBIP Funds** - Transitional Bilingual Instruction Program
- **LAP Funds** - Learning Assistance Program
- **BEST Grants** - Beginning Educator Support Team
- **Title II and Title III** federal funds

## Federal Title Funding (Applies to All States)

### Title II Requirements

Title II funds must be used to address the learning needs of English learners (Sec. 2103(b)(2)) and can support programs that "increase the ability of teachers to effectively teach . . . English learners" (Sec. 2103(b)(3)(F)).

### Title III Requirements

Title III **requires** the use of funds "to provide effective professional development to classroom teachers (including teachers in classroom settings that are not the settings of language instruction educational programs), principals and other school leaders, administrators, and other school or community-based organizational personnel" (Sec. 3115(c)(2)).

**CARE Coaching is an allowable expenditure using Title II and Title III funds.**

## Targeting Recommendations

### Priority States for CARE Coaching Outreach

1. **Florida** - Highest priority due to:
   - Explicit hour requirements (60-300 hours)
   - June 2026 compliance deadline for district matrices
   - CSG funding readily available
   - Clear opportunity to develop 60-hour aligned course

2. **Colorado** - High priority due to:
   - WCEPS already approved as PD provider
   - Clear 45-hour requirement with defined educator categories
   - Strong state funding through ELPA

3. **Washington** - Moderate priority due to:
   - 100 clock hour requirement creates ongoing demand
   - Multiple funding sources (TBIP, LAP, BEST)
   - Requires becoming approved provider for maximum impact

### Key Contacts to Target

- Superintendents
- ESOL/ELL Directors
- Curriculum Directors/Leaders
- Title III Coordinators
- Federal Programs Directors
- CSI/TSI/ATSI School Principals (Florida)
